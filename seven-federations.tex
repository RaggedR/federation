\section{The structure}

Each member \textbf{has} a container and \textbf{is} a morphism, and keeping those
apart is what makes the rest legible.

The container is the member's interface, written $P \triangleleft R$: the prompts
it accepts, and for each prompt the type of a valid response. A tribe accepts
\emph{muster}, \emph{contribute}, \emph{adjudicate}. A satrapy accepts
\emph{tribute}, \emph{levy}, \emph{keep the peace}. A state accepts
\emph{requisition}. A firm accepts an order.

The morphism is the member's behaviour. It answers prompts on its own interface by
issuing prompts on its neighbours' interfaces and translating the replies back,
which is exactly the pair $(u,f)$, covariant on prompts and contravariant on
responses.

So a federation is not a composite of containers. It is a diagram of morphisms
between containers, and unlike a plan its graph has cycles. The four combinators
build trees.

\begin{sectionbox}
The failure is entirely in the response direction. Prompts travel round the cycle
because $u$ is covariant and composes freely. Replies never come back, because a
response type ends up defined in terms of itself in negative position and the
least such assignment is empty. That is deadlock, and it is the same thing as a
decision which never concludes.
\end{sectionbox}

\section{Persia}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A satrapy. Prompts are \emph{tribute}, \emph{levy}, \emph{keep
the peace}. Responses are silver, soldiers, and the absence of revolt.

\textbf{The response type is deliberately thin.} Nothing in it describes how the
silver was raised, which law was applied, or which god was worshipped. That
thinness is the abstraction barrier, and it is the entire design.

\textbf{Composition.} $\boxtimes$ across satrapies. They run in parallel and do not
consume each other's interfaces, so no cycle ever forms and the deadlock question
never arises.
\end{sectionbox}

The Achaemenid empire ran twenty-odd satrapies for two hundred and twenty years.
Darius fixed the annual tribute per satrapy in advance, in silver, and Herodotus
records the schedule. The tribute was predictable rather than arbitrary, and that
single fact is what separates the arrangement from a racket.

Tilly's argument is that states and racketeers both sell protection against a
danger they largely manufacture, and that legitimacy is granted retrospectively by
success and duration. The distinction that rescues Persia is Olson's. A roving
bandit takes everything and moves on. A stationary bandit knows he will be back
next year, so he takes a sustainable share and acquires a real interest in the
productivity of his subjects. A long time horizon converts predation into
governance. A racket does not build roads, and Persia built the royal road.

\textbf{Contrast with Gosplan.} Same combinator, same topology, opposite
discipline. Gosplan's prompts reach into the implementation and its response type
tries to carry the interior back, and it drowned in reports that terror had already
made worthless. Persia asked for a value at the boundary and refused to look behind
it.

\textbf{The audit was built in.} Satraps governed, but garrison commanders and
treasury officials frequently reported to the king on separate lines, and royal
inspectors travelled. Two channels which ought to agree is precisely the cross
check.

\textbf{The cost of the design.} An abstraction barrier makes the implementation
swappable. Alexander took the empire largely by leaving the satrapies intact and
replacing only the root, and from below nothing distinguished the new king from the
old one. Specify only the interface and you gain two centuries of stability and
lose the ability to prevent your own substitution.

\section{Abraham}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A tribe. Prompts are \emph{muster}, \emph{contribute},
\emph{adjudicate}. Responses are men, goods, and a judgement.

\textbf{The cycle.} Tribe $A$'s response to \emph{adjudicate} depends on $B$'s,
$B$'s on $C$'s, and $C$'s on $A$'s. The forward map composes freely and the
backward map never returns.

\textbf{Why a dead ancestor terminates it.} The ancestor is a container whose
responses are already determined and depend on nothing. Formally he is a constant,
and a constant is well founded by definition. The recursion bottoms out in him
because there is nothing left to compute.
\end{sectionbox}

The problem with federating tribes is that no tribe will accept another tribe as
the root, because that is submission and not federation. The root has to lie
outside the set of current members. There are two directions in which to put it.
Above, which is one god. Or behind, which is a common ancestor.

A segmentary system is a dependent composition. Me against my brother, my brother
and I against my cousin, the three of us against the stranger. The group which
coheres is a function of who is opposing it, so the composite re-roots itself per
conflict. There is always a root and it is never the same one twice.

A common ancestor lets that structure close at the top. Keep widening the circle of
opposition and eventually every member of the federation is kin. Twelve tribes from
Jacob. The Arab lines through Ishmael.

\textbf{The membership token is costly and irreversible.} Genesis 17 attaches
circumcision to the covenant. Whatever else it is, it is a marker which cannot be
faked and cannot be undone, which is the opposite of cheap exit.

\textbf{The honest complication.} Anthropologically the genealogies are adjusted.
Segmentary lineages get rewritten to reflect the alliances which actually exist,
which means the common ancestor is frequently the effect of the federation rather
than its cause. That is not a debunking. It is the more interesting version,
because it says a federation invents its own root when it needs one and puts it in
the past where nobody can contest it.

Noth's amphictyony hypothesis of 1930, reading the twelve tribes as a cultic league
on the Greek model, is now largely rejected. The underlying observation does not
depend on it.

\section{Muhammad}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A tribe, as above. The umma is the composite.

\textbf{Why this root is different.} Abraham is a constant. Muhammad is a container
whose responses are still being computed, because he is alive and can be asked new
questions. The fixpoint is well defined only while the computation runs.
\end{sectionbox}

The Constitution of Medina, 622, is the clearest federation document anywhere. It
binds the Muhajirun, the Ansar and named Jewish tribes into one umma, explicitly to
the exclusion of other people.

What makes it a federation rather than an absorption is that the tribes keep their
internal structure. Blood money obligations continue to run within each tribe. The
document unifies the outward interface while leaving the implementation alone,
which is information hiding written as a treaty in 622, and the Persian move
arrived at independently in a single generation rather than over centuries.

Ibn Khaldun supplies the mechanism. Tribal solidarity is naturally bounded by
lineage, and religion is what carries it past that bound, because it lets people
who are not kin owe each other what kin owe each other. \emph{Asabiyya} is the
scarce resource in a federation, and a shared root above manufactures more of it
than kinship can supply. The 1744 pact between Muhammad bin Saud and Muhammad ibn
Abd al-Wahhab is the worked example.

\textbf{The succession problem.} Muhammad died in 632 and the tribes considered
their allegiance to have been personal to him, so it lapsed. The Ridda wars of 632
and 633 were Abu Bakr establishing by force that the federation outlived its
founder.

That is the cost of this repair stated exactly. A root outside the set is what
makes federation possible, and a living person is only temporarily outside the set.
He dies, and the root must be re-established or transferred, and transferring it
means somebody inside the set now holds it. Abraham does not have this problem
because he was already dead when they started. A constitution does not have it
either.

\section{Putin}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} In the 1990s, an oligarch or a governor, with a genuinely
autonomous interface and a response type which could return \emph{no}. The graph
had cycles and Yeltsin was a weak root arbitrating between them. That was a real
confederation.

\textbf{What the vertical did.} Every node's interface was rewritten so its response
type became whatever the apex required. The forward map still runs. The backward
map now returns a constant.

\textbf{And a constant carries no information.} When every response is determined by
the apex rather than by the state of the world, the mutual information between the
true state and the report goes to zero. The channel is perfectly reliable and
completely useless.
\end{sectionbox}

Calling Russia a confederation of criminal interests gets the criminal half right
and the confederation half exactly backwards, and the backwards half is the
instructive one.

\textbf{The criminal half is well supported.} \emph{Krysha}, literally roof, is the
Russian word for both a gang's protection and a patron's political cover, and one
word doing both jobs is not a coincidence. \emph{Reiderstvo} is a protection racket
with a courthouse attached. Killing defectors abroad is the informer rule. Dawisha
argues the arrangement was built for accumulation from the St Petersburg years
onward rather than being a state which later went bad. Galeotti puts the careful
version: Russia is not run by the mafia, the state subordinated organised crime and
uses it as an instrument.

\textbf{The confederation half describes the country Putin dismantled.} A
confederation is sovereign units voluntarily associated with a weak centre derived
from its members, and that was Russia in the 1990s. The entire project after 2000
was its negation. Gusinsky and Berezovsky driven out, Khodorkovsky jailed and Yukos
destroyed in 2003, governors appointed rather than elected from 2004, and the thing
named openly as the vertical of power. Prigozhin settles it: the one man who
behaved as though he were a sovereign partner was dead two months after acting on
the assumption.

The right description is a patrimonial hierarchy in Weber's sense, with no line
drawn between the ruler's household and the state, and property held conditionally
on loyalty and revocable at the apex.

\textbf{What it cost.} A gang boss hears the truth, because the organisation is
small and his people are close. A patrimonial sovereign is surrounded by people who
hold everything at his pleasure. Becoming the ultimate gangster destroys the exact
informational conditions which made you a competent one, and the reporting on
Ukraine which reached the top in early 2022 was reportedly what the top wanted to
hear.

\textbf{Where the criminal model stops working.} A gangster is a rational profit
maximiser, and the model stops predicting in 2022. Invading Ukraine was
catastrophic for exactly the interests a racket exists to protect. No racketeer
burns down his own territory, so something other than accumulation was driving it.

\section{USA}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A state. Prompts are \emph{requisition}, \emph{muster},
\emph{enforce}. Responses are money, troops, compliance.

\textbf{The Articles' defect stated exactly.} The response type of
\emph{requisition} admitted a null. It returned a \texttt{Maybe} and Congress
needed a value. No amount of prompting fixes a response type which is allowed to be
empty, which is why eight years of requisitions produced eight years of nothing.
\end{sectionbox}

Under the Articles, Congress could requisition money from the states but could not
tax. Amendment required unanimity, so Rhode Island alone killed the impost of 1781
and New York killed the next one. No executive, no judiciary, and states levying
tariffs against each other. Prompts propagated freely for eight years and replies
did not.

\textbf{1787 applies two repairs crudely and a third elegantly.} The Supremacy
Clause chooses a root. Ratification by nine of thirteen rather than thirteen of
thirteen is bounded unrolling, taking the approximation rather than waiting for a
fixpoint which will never arrive. And then staggered elections, six year Senate
terms with a third turning over each cycle, an amendment process measured in years,
litigation measured in years. Every turn of the feedback loop is forced to cost a
step. That is guarded recursion, and the delay is not friction in the design. The
delay is what makes it terminate.

\textbf{What actually changed} was not merely supremacy. The federal government
acquired its own interface directly onto the citizen, rather than composing through
the states as intermediaries. Federal law binds people, not member governments,
which removes the state from the response path for the things that matter. That is
why the null disappeared.

\textbf{Dred Scott.} The prompt is \emph{what is this person's legal status}. Under
a federation of divergent state law the response depended on the path travelled, so
it was not a function. A response type which is path dependent is not a type, and
the composite had no fixpoint. The war was the system failing to have one.

\textbf{Present tense}, it moves both ways at once. Centralised through the income
tax, the Commerce Clause after \emph{Wickard}, and conditional spending which buys
compliance rather than commanding it. Re-federalising through the anti-commandeering
doctrine, cannabis being federally illegal and openly sold, sanctuary jurisdictions,
California setting emissions standards the market then follows, and abortion law
diverging by state after \emph{Dobbs}.

\section{EU}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A member state, with a genuinely thick interface. Unlike an
American state it retains its own foreign policy, its own tax base, and an exit.

\textbf{Unanimity is the Articles' defect, restored on purpose.} In the unanimity
domains the composite requires every member's response to be affirmative, so a
single null annihilates the result. Hungary is not exploiting a loophole. It is
returning the value the type permits.
\end{sectionbox}

Every other case here resolved the question one way or the other. The EU is the
live one, and all four repairs are visible at once.

\textbf{Unroll.} Qualified majority voting is the approximation taken rather than
the fixpoint awaited. Fifty five per cent of states representing sixty five per cent
of the population, and the holdouts are outvoted. It covers most single market
legislation and pointedly not foreign policy, taxation or enlargement.

\textbf{Orient, contested.} The supremacy of EU law was asserted by the Court in
\emph{Costa v ENEL} and \emph{Van Gend en Loos}, not written plainly into the
founding treaties. The Court chose a root. National constitutional courts have
contested it ever since, through the German \emph{Solange} line and the PSPP ruling
of 2020, and the Polish Constitutional Tribunal in 2021. A diagram with two proposed
roots and no agreement is exactly a federation which has not chosen.

\textbf{Guard.} The EU runs on delay more than any other institution here. Multi
year legislative processes, transition periods, review clauses, and the shadow of a
vote not yet taken. Guarded recursion as an operating culture.

\textbf{Commute.} The single market is the commuting part, which is why it succeeded
first and most completely. Standards, mutual recognition and free movement do not
depend on the order in which members adopt them.

\textbf{Where it does not commute.} The euro is monetary policy centralised without
fiscal policy centralised. Two morphisms writing to the same value with no
sequencing between them. The crisis of 2010 to 2012 was the federation unable to
decide, and Draghi saying whatever it takes in 2012 was the centre acting beyond a
mandate nobody had given it because the alternative was deadlock. NextGenerationEU
in 2020 was the first large common borrowing and was widely called a Hamiltonian
moment, which is a direct reference to the assumption of state debts in 1790.
Whether it was a precedent or a one off is the open question.

\textbf{What makes it more confederal than the United States} is Article 50. Exit
exists and has been used. \emph{Texas v.\ White} closed that in America in 1869.

\section{Mergers and acquisitions}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{Container.} A firm. Prompts come from customers, suppliers and capital.
Responses are products, deliveries and cash.

\textbf{A merger of equals is a composite with two roots, which is no root.} The
prompts requiring a single answer, meaning which system, which process, which
person, have two well typed responses and no rule for choosing. The composite is
not a function.
\end{sectionbox}

DaimlerChrysler in 1998 was sold as a merger of equals and was a Daimler
acquisition. Two headquarters, two cultures, no agreed root, and it was unwound in
2007 at enormous cost. The phrase is now close to a warning label, and the reason is
structural rather than cultural.

\textbf{Dual parent structures can last, and they end the same way.} Unilever ran as
NV and PLC from 1930 until it unified in 2020. Royal Dutch Shell ran dual parent
from 1907 until 2005. Rio Tinto is still dual listed. Ninety years is not a failure
by any standard, but the direction of travel is consistent, which is the same
profile Ibn Khaldun gives for \emph{asabiyya}.

\textbf{Integration is choosing the spanning tree.} Whose systems, whose processes,
whose reporting lines, whose people. Everybody involved knows exactly what is at
stake, which is why it is the part that goes wrong.

\textbf{The federation that works declines to integrate.} Berkshire runs roughly
twenty five people at headquarters over a group employing several hundred thousand.
The interface is \emph{send the cash to Omaha and do not lose money}, and the
response type carries nothing about the interior. That is Persia: same repair, same
abstraction barrier, same durability bought by giving up control of implementation.

\textbf{Two smaller mechanisms.} Earnouts and deferred consideration are the guard
repair, since payment is contingent and delayed so neither party can demand the
fixpoint today. And antitrust is a root placed above the federation of firms by
something none of them is.

\textbf{The commutativity test predicts the outcome.} Bolt-ons into separate markets
commute, so integration is nearly free. Two salesforces, two ERP systems and two
head offices are two implementations of one interface, and only one can survive.

\section{Two claims}

\begin{sectionbox}
\textbf{First.} A federation is well typed exactly where its operations commute, and
grows a centre exactly where they do not.

Building a bakery commutes with building another bakery. Allocating the last of the
steel does not. Slavery in one state and freedom in another do not commute once a
person moves, which is the entire content of \emph{Dred Scott}.
\end{sectionbox}

\begin{alarmbox}
\textbf{Second.} The root must lie outside the set of members. No tribe accepts
another tribe as sovereign, because that is submission rather than federation.

One god works because none of them is it. A common ancestor works because he is
dead. A written constitution works because it is not a person. Where the root is
inside the set, as with Putin and with most mergers, the federation is already over
and the paperwork has not caught up.
\end{alarmbox}
